How to read the labels

HS ONLY
Built for middle or high school students. College teams cannot enter.
HS DIVISION
Open to many players, but high schoolers are named in the rules and get their own division, bracket, or scoreboard.
OPEN TO ALL
Anyone may register and play. There is no high school prize bracket, so students compete in the open division for experience.
CONFIRMED
The organizers have published this year's dates.
EXPECTED
Dates are not announced yet. The month is based on when the event was last held. Dashed cards update automatically when dates are posted.

Before registering, read each competition's rules. Most require players to be at least 13, and players under 18 usually need a parent or guardian's consent. College-only competitions are left off this list on purpose.
