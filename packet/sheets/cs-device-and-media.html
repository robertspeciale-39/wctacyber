Devices, Cables & Link Lights

Pick the right box, pick the right wire, read the light before you open a CLI.

Device selection

The one that gets tested A switch cannot reach a different network. Count the networks in the requirement. Two or more means a router, no matter what the picture looks like.

Interface naming

  • F0/1 FastEthernet, 100 Mbps
  • G0/0 GigabitEthernet, 1 Gbps
  • S0/0/0 Serial, WAN link
  • Vlan1 Switch management interface

In G0/1, the 0 is the module or slot and the 1 is the port on it. Routers usually number from 0. Switch access ports usually start at F0/1.

Cable selection

PC → any device's console portConsole (rollover)

The rule underneath the table: same kind of device, crossover. Different kinds, straight-through. A router and a PC are both "end" devices for this purpose, which is why that one surprises people.

Attenuation is distance. EMI/RFI is the environment. Crosstalk is the cable itself. Three words, three different causes.

Link lights: read this first, always

LightMeansDo this
● GreenLink is upPhysical layer is fine. The problem is configuration.
● AmberPort is blocking, or still coming upWait a moment. On a switch this is usually spanning tree.
● RedLink downInterface is administratively down. Check no shutdown.
● DarkNothing connectedWrong port, wrong cable type, or no cable.
Habit worth points The link light is your first diagnostic. Nobody has ever fixed a dark link by typing commands into it.
