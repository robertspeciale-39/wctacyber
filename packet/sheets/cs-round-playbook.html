Round Playbook

How to spend the clock. Nothing on this page is networking knowledge, and it is worth more points than most of what is.

Before you touch anything

  1. Read every task first. All of them. You cannot triage a list you have not read, and the last task is often the easiest one.
  2. Sort by points per minute. A five-point task you can do in one minute beats a fifteen-point task that takes twenty.
  3. Note the tasks that depend on other tasks. Addressing before routing, routing before services. Doing them out of order means doing them twice.
  4. Budget the quiz at 20 minutes and get into the lab.

The fixed configuration order

Use this order every single time, in practice and in competition. It is not the fastest possible order. It is the order that makes skipping a step obvious.

#StepYou are done when
1Topology: place devices, pick cablesEvery link light is green
2Addressing: every interface and hostipconfig and show ip interface brief match your plan
3Gateways: on every hostEach host pings its own gateway
4Routing: static and default routesshow ip route lists every remote network
5Services: DHCP, DNS, HTTPA client proves it, not the server
6Verification: end to endPing and tracert across the whole path

While you work

Check the score as you go

Open the Activity Wizard completion percentage after each device, not at the end. A task you thought you finished and did not is cheap to fix at minute 12 and expensive at minute 55.

Never leave a device unsaved

copy running-config startup-config before you move on. Packet Tracer crashes. It always crashes at the worst moment.

When something does not work

Stop. Run the diagnostic sequence instead of changing settings at random. Randomly changing settings is how a broken network becomes two broken networks.

When you are stuck for three minutes

Leave it. Move to the next task, come back. Three minutes of staring is worth more spent on points you can actually get.

The last five minutes

  • Save every device again.
  • Re-read the task list and check off each item against what you actually built.
  • Run one end-to-end ping from the furthest host to the furthest host.
  • Check the completion percentage one final time.

After the round: name the loss

Every point you did not get falls into exactly one of three buckets. Say which, out loud, before you leave.

Knowledge You did not know it. Go back to the module.
Speed You knew it and ran out of clock. Go drill it.
Process You skipped a task, misread it, or forgot to save. Come back to this page.
